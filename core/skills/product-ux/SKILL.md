# Product UX

Load the current project's product and architecture hooks. Define lightweight success criteria, the user's real goal, primary and secondary tasks, required information, decisions, feedback, cancellation, recovery, and consequential confirmation.

For concept work, inspect the current implementation for capabilities and constraints, then deliberately set its layout aside. Decide what is immediate, secondary, progressively disclosed, moved, or removed before choosing components or styling. Explore alternatives internally and present more than one only for a meaningful structural tradeoff.

Hand off the chosen hierarchy, behavior, states, removed/deferred elements, and unresolved product decisions. Never invent business rules.
